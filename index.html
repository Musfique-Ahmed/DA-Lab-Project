01 · Business Problem & Domain

The cost of getting it wrong, on both sides of a loan application.

Lenders lose money when high-risk applicants are approved and forfeit revenue when creditworthy applicants are rejected. Manual underwriting cannot scale or stay consistent across volume. A statistical risk model — and ultimately an AI agent — closes that gap.

01 Domain

Banking & Fintech

Consumer lending and credit risk — a domain where even a small lift in default prediction translates directly into measurable financial impact.

02 Problem statement

Forecast default at application time

How can a lender accurately forecast the probability of loan default at the time of application? Rejecting too many creditworthy applicants forfeits revenue; approving high-risk applicants drives default losses; manual underwriting cannot scale or stay consistent across volume.

03 Business value

What good looks like

  • Reduce default losses through earlier, sharper risk detection
  • Approve more creditworthy applicants with confidence
  • Consistent, explainable scoring across volume
  • Faster, scalable underwriting decisioning

03 · Preprocessing & EDA Strategy

Planned data preparation and exploratory analysis.

A repeatable flow from raw CSV to model-ready features, plus the questions and hypotheses that will guide exploration.

Preprocessing flow

1

Load primary & auxiliary tables

Read application_train.csv plus 7 auxiliary tables and the column-description reference file.

2

Clean the placeholder anomaly

Replace DAYS_EMPLOYED == 365243 with NaN.

3

Drop high-missing columns

Remove columns with > 50% missing (e.g. building / apartment attributes).

4

Impute remaining nulls

Median for numerics; mode or "Unknown" for categoricals.

5

Encode categoricals

One-hot for low-cardinality; target / frequency encoding for high-cardinality.

6

Scale numerics

StandardScaler or RobustScaler to stabilise model input distributions.

EDA questions

Four business questions the analysis will surface.

  1. Q1Default rate vs. credit-to-income ratio
  2. Q2External bureau scores (EXT_SOURCE_1/2/3) vs. default outcome
  3. Q3Employment length and income stability vs. risk
  4. Q4Default rate across education, occupation, and contract type

Hypotheses

Three falsifiable claims the EDA will test.

  1. H1Lower EXT_SOURCE scores correlate with a higher probability of default.
  2. H2A higher credit-to-income ratio increases default likelihood.
  3. H3Certain occupation and education segments show measurably elevated risk.
04 · Feature Selection & Modeling Strategy

From 122 raw features to four candidate models and a decisioning agent.

Establish an interpretable baseline, then layer in ensembles and a deep-learning comparison point, all feeding a single AI agent for end users.

Feature selection

Rank → reduce → compare

Plan

Rank all 122 features using Random Forest / XGBoost feature importances and SHAP values; retain the top ~30. Compare a baseline model (all features) against the reduced-feature model on validation AUC.
